How scoring works

01

Fencing

In qualification, winning 70% of your bouts = 250 points. In semi-finals and finals it’s direct elimination, and 1st place = 250.

02

Obstacle

Faster is better: every second = 3 points. A 30-second run scores 355.

03

Swimming

100m freestyle. 1:10.00 = 250 points, and every second faster or slower is worth 5 points.

04

Laser Run

Shooting and running combined. 10:00 = 700 points, and every second is worth 1 point.
